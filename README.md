IMDM101 Assignment 4

A short Halloween horror game made with Unity. You play as a character who's stuck in the dark
in a foggy rural area at night, where you have to collect all six pies while being chased. You 
can use your flashlight to find all the pies before the enemy catches you. Either collect all pies
or get caught and lose.
